GIV.UK – Claim 20% off work (build k7q)
Fictional training service for an accessibility awareness course.

Open giv-k7q-01-start.html in a browser. Keep all files and the assets folder together.
Works offline. No external dependencies. Answers are stored in sessionStorage for this tab only.

Pages:
  giv-k7q-01-start.html         Introduction (barrier: inaccessible 60-second time limit)
  giv-k7q-02-details.html       Enter your details (barrier: missing form labels)
  giv-k7q-03-pattern.html       Select your working pattern (barrier: not keyboard accessible)
  giv-k7q-04-coupon.html        Enter a coupon code (barrier: instructions by colour alone)
  giv-k7q-05-submit.html        Submit your claim (barrier: unclear error messages)
  giv-k7q-06-confirmation.html  Confirmation (barrier: insufficient contrast)
  giv-k7q-expired.html          Offer expired (continue without time limit)
  giv-k7q-about.html            About the activity, barriers and WCAG 2.2 references
