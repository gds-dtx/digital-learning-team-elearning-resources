About this training activity

GIV.UK is a made-up service for an accessibility awareness course. It is not a real government service.

What the activity is for

You try to claim a coupon to work 20% fewer hours. Along the way, each page has one deliberate accessibility barrier. The aim is to experience how poor digital design can exclude disabled people.

This is not a disability simulation. It does not interfere with any assistive technology. The barriers are design decisions that teams make every day, and they can be fixed.

Completing the activity in a way that works for you

  • On every page, a “Training layer” box lets you show the accessible version of that page.
  • You can remove the 60-second time limit at any point. Your answers are kept.
  • The accessible route from the start page has no time limit and shows the accessible version of every page first.
