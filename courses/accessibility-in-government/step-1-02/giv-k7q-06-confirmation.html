About the barrier on this page: insufficient colour contrast
What the barrier is
Your coupon reference – the most important information on the page – is light pink (#EE6FCB) on a pink panel (#DE00A5).
What happens
The contrast ratio is about 1.7:1. Many people will struggle to read the reference, or not notice it at all, especially on a phone outdoors or a dim screen.
Who it can affect
Partially sighted people, older people, people with colour vision deficiency, and anyone in bright light or using a low-quality screen.
WCAG 2.2
1.4.3 Contrast (Minimum) (AA). Text needs a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1, or 3:1 for large text.
How the accessible version fixes it
The reference is white (#FFFFFF) on the same panel, a contrast ratio of about 4.5:1, and it is large, bold text.
