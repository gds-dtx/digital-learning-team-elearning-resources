About this activity

GIV.UK is a made-up service for an accessibility awareness course. It is not a real government service.

Each step has one design problem built in on purpose. After you try each step, a short pop-up explains what happened and who it affects.

If you get stuck

If a step gets in your way, a pop-up will offer to show you a fixed version of the page or let you skip it. You can also start without a time limit, or use the accessible version from the start page.
