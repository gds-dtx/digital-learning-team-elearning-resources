    About the barrier on this page: controls you cannot use with a keyboard
    What the barrier is
    The option cards and the Continue control are <div> elements that only respond to a mouse click or tap. They have no keyboard focus, no key handling and no role or state.
    What happens
    Press Tab: focus skips straight past the options and Continue. You cannot select a working pattern or move on without a mouse or touch screen. Screen readers do not identify the cards as choices, or say which one is selected.
    Who it can affect
    Keyboard-only users, switch and voice control users, screen reader users, and some people with tremors or limited dexterity.
    WCAG 2.2
    2.1.1 Keyboard (A); 4.1.2 Name, Role, Value (A).
    How the accessible version fixes it
    Native radio buttons inside a <fieldset> with a <legend>, and a real <button>. They work with Tab, the arrow keys, Space and Enter, and expose their role and checked state.
