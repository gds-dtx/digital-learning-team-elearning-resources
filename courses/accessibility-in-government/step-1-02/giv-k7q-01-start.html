Training layer

Need more time, or an accessible route?

Every page of this service has one deliberate accessibility barrier, so you can experience how poor design excludes people. You can always switch any page to an accessible version, and remove the time limit.

If you prefer, start on the accessible route. There is no time limit and the accessible version of every page is shown first.

About this training activity

About the barrier on this page: an inaccessible time limit
What the barrier is
A fixed 60-second countdown for the whole service, with no way within the service to turn it off, adjust it or extend it.
What happens
If the countdown reaches zero before you submit, the offer expires and you are sent to an “offer expired” page.
Who it can affect
People who use screen readers, switch or voice control, or screen magnifiers; people with reading, memory or attention difficulties; people with tremors or fatigue; anyone who is interrupted.
WCAG 2.2
2.2.1 Timing Adjustable (Level A). Users must be able to turn off, adjust (at least 10 times the default) or extend a time limit, unless an exception applies.
How the accessible version fixes it
The accessible route and the “Remove the 60-second time limit” button switch the timer off. Your answers are kept.
